# Stage 7 — Historical legal scope clarification (2026-11-18)

Study: `ARHRN-INDEPENDENT-BRIDAL-SPECIAL-SUPPLY-REFORM-20261108`.

**Important reader warning:** The 18% → 23% change concerns the **2025-03-31 historical amendment**. It must **not** be represented as the current rate for every 2026 private-apartment subscription notice. The 2026-06-15 version of Article 41(1) displayed **15%** for newlywed special supply in the private-housing provision. For current applications, consult the relevant **current announcement and rules**.

## Confirmed 2025 rule
- March 31, 2025 Ministerial Rule No.1471 amended Article 41 from **18% to 23%**.
- Transitional provision Article 3 applies the former provisions where an announcement was made **before** the rule took effect.
- Thus the 2025 dataset's **announcement date** is a defensible historical grouping variable for a descriptive comparison; this does **not** show each project's ultimate legal compliance or identify the reform's causal effect.

## Unresolved hierarchy/administrative-text issue
The separate March 31, 2025 `신혼부부 주택 특별공급 운용지침` (Notice 2025-160), Article 3(1)(1), continued to display **18%**. The precise administrative/legal reconciliation has not been established; the discrepancy should **not be omitted** or "corrected" without a competent official source.

## Boundary case audit
Ten notices originally reviewed at Stage 4 were rechecked against the 502-notice internal panel for announcement date, eligible supply and newlywed counts. All ten joins are consistent. Eight PDFs had been recorded as found/checked in earlier stages and two remained unverified. **No new independently downloaded PDF or original approval-application date was secured at Stage 7**, so this is not a new verification of individual compliance.
