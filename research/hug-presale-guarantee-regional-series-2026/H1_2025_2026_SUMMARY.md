Data published: 2026-10-09. Based on official HUG issuance data and Resimanor's verified cleaned CSV.

### Headline observation

- 2025 H1: 202,579억원 issuance, 45,023 households.
- 2026 H1: 431,426억원 issuance, 90,160 households.

Because the numbers of region-quarter rows differ, matched-panel analysis is preferred:
- **29 matching province-quarter pairs** across both years.
- 2025 amount: 202,601억원.
- 2026 amount: 426,340억원.
- Difference: **+223,739억원**.
- Matched households: 45,023 → 88,896; difference **+43,873**.
