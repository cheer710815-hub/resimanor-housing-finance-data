Caveats:
- This is a preservation and arithmetic transformation of official observations, **not** independent measurements or evidence of causal effects.
- Negative source values, omitted region-quarter rows and ambiguous `전라` are documented.
- Issuance amounts per household are **not** dwelling sale prices.
- 2026 is half-year only.
- Versioned GitHub commits offer fixed citations. Issue a DOI only after a verified archival deposit.
