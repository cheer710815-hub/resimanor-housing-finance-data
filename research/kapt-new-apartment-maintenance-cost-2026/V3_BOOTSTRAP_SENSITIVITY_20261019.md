# N02 v3: exploratory within-sample bootstrap of median component changes (2026-10-19)

Input: archived 60-complex source-recomputed `component_comparison.csv`; strict eligibility requires first three and final three observations of each field to be numeric and strictly positive. Bootstrap: 20,000 percentile resamples, sampled with replacement from eligible complexes within each comparison group; deterministic seed 20261019. Summary generated by local standard-library script `n02_bootstrap_uncertainty.py`.

| Field | Eligible n | Observed sample median % | Bootstrap 2.5th percentile % | Bootstrap 97.5th percentile % | Positive / negative complexes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| pay | 50 | +1.93 | -2.94 | +3.93 | 28 / 22 |
| cleanCost | 50 | +2.24 | -4.31 | +4.70 | 27 / 23 |
| guardCost | 47 | +1.86 | -3.17 | +3.93 | 26 / 21 |

All three resampling intervals include zero. However these **are not nationwide or probability-sampling confidence intervals**. The selected complexes are purposive, missingness is nonrandom, and calendar-period, region and building size composition vary. This sensitivity analysis quantifies instability of the observed sample medians only, and does not establish a statistical test, causal effect, or general population trend.
