# Methodology — New Mortgage 5% + Existing Monthly Debt Service 500k KRW

For each income × region scenario, the source dataset's theoretical DSR-based new mortgage limit was selected under:
- actual new mortgage rate = 5.0%
- existing monthly debt service = 500,000 KRW

The new mortgage monthly payment was calculated as a 30-year equal-principal-and-interest payment at the actual 5.0% rate.

Total monthly debt service after the new mortgage is:

existing monthly debt service (500,000 KRW) + new mortgage monthly repayment.

The calculation therefore separates:
1. the amount already being paid before the new mortgage, and
2. the additional monthly burden created by the new mortgage.
