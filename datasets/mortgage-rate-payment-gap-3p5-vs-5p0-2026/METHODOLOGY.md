# Methodology

For each loan balance and remaining term, monthly payment was calculated twice using the standard amortizing-loan formula.

- Scenario A: 3.5% annual mortgage rate
- Scenario B: 5.0% annual mortgage rate
- Equal principal-and-interest repayment

Derived fields:
- monthly increase = payment at 5.0% - payment at 3.5%
- annual increase = monthly increase × 12
- total payment increase over term = monthly increase × number of remaining months

This is a standardized fixed-rate comparison for understanding payment sensitivity. It is not a forecast of future rates or a lender quote.
