# Mortgage-rate payment gap: 3.5% vs 5.0% (2026)

## Purpose
This dataset is designed to answer a different search intent from Resimanor's existing mortgage articles.

It does **not** compare repayment methods and it does **not** calculate DSR borrowing limits.

It asks one narrow cash-flow question:

> For the same mortgage principal and the same remaining term, how much more is the monthly payment at 5.0% than at 3.5%?

## Why this is separate from existing Resimanor posts
- Post #726 compares **3억원 at 4%** and focuses on **equal-payment vs equal-principal repayment methods**.
- Post #1229 compares **3.5% vs 5.0%** but focuses on **DSR-based borrowing limits**, not monthly cash flow.
- Post #976 focuses on **rate-reduction requests** and the savings from a 0.3%p decrease.

This dataset instead compares the **payment shock from a 1.5%p mortgage-rate gap** across multiple loan balances and remaining terms.

## Assumptions
- Mortgage rate A: 3.5%
- Mortgage rate B: 5.0%
- Repayment: equal principal + interest
- Loan balance: 200M, 300M, 400M, 500M KRW
- Remaining term: 10, 20, 30 years
- No fees, prepayment, insurance, or rate resets

## Key examples
- 2억원, 30년: monthly +18만원, annual +211만원
- 3억원, 30년: monthly +26만원, annual +316만원
- 4억원, 30년: monthly +35만원, annual +421만원
- 5억원, 30년: monthly +44만원, annual +527만원

The shorter the remaining term, the larger the monthly payment itself, but the effect of the rate gap on total lifetime payments differs by term.

## Intended article angle
**신규 주담대 금리 5%대, 기존 3%대보다 월 부담 얼마나 늘까? 대출잔액·기간별 비교**

The article should be organized around **monthly/annual cash-flow burden**, not DSR limits and not repayment-method choice.
