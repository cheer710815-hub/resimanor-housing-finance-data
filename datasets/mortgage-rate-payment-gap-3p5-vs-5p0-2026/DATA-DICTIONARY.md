# Data Dictionary

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| loan_principal_krw | Same mortgage balance used in both rate scenarios |
| term_years | Remaining repayment term |
| monthly_payment_3_5pct_krw | Monthly equal payment at 3.5% |
| monthly_payment_5_0pct_krw | Monthly equal payment at 5.0% |
| monthly_increase_krw | Additional monthly burden at 5.0% |
| annual_increase_krw | Additional annual burden |
| total_payment_increase_over_term_krw | Additional total payments if each rate stayed fixed for the full remaining term |
