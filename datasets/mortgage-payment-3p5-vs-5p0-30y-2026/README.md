# Mortgage Payment Comparison: 3.5% vs 5.0% — 30-Year Equal Payment

## Research question
If a borrower takes the same mortgage principal over 30 years with equal principal-and-interest payments, how much more is paid each month when the rate is 5.0% instead of 3.5%?

## Assumptions
- Loan principal: 100M to 500M KRW
- Term: 30 years
- Repayment method: equal principal + interest
- Rate comparison: 3.5% vs 5.0%
- No fees, insurance, prepayment, or changing rate path included

## Key results
- 100M KRW: +87,777 KRW/month
- 200M KRW: +175,554 KRW/month
- 300M KRW: +263,331 KRW/month
- 400M KRW: +351,108 KRW/month
- 500M KRW: +438,885 KRW/month

At the same principal and term, the monthly payment is about 19.55% higher at 5.0% than at 3.5%.

## Interpretation
This dataset focuses on monthly cash-flow burden, not DSR borrowing limits.
