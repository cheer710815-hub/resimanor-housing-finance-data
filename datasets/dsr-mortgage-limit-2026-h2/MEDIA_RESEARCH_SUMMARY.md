# Research / Media Summary

## Main finding
Under controlled assumptions, KRW 80m annual income with KRW 1m of monthly-equivalent existing DSR debt service can leave the same new-mortgage DSR capacity as KRW 50m annual income with no existing DSR burden.

At a 4.0% mortgage rate, 30-year equal principal-and-interest repayment and the metropolitan/regulatory 3.0% stress-rate scenario, both calculate to about KRW 250.51m.

## Existing-debt effect
For KRW 50m annual income under the same 4.0% / 30-year / 3.0% stress-rate scenario, moving from zero existing burden to KRW 500k monthly equivalent reduces the calculated new-mortgage limit by about KRW 75.15m.

## Citation wording
Use “DSR-based calculated limit” or “DSR-based theoretical mortgage limit.” Do not describe these figures as actual approved loan amounts.

The 0.75% value in the second regional scenario is a controlled analytical reference, not a universal fixed official rate.
