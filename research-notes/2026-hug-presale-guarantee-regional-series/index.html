South Korea Presale Guarantee Issuance, 2009–2026H1: A Regional Time-Series Data Note

Resimanor Housing Finance Research Note No. 2
Author: Eun Kim · Affiliation: Resimanor · ORCID: 0009-0006-9445-4768 · Release date: 2026-10-10

Working paper / research note. This note is not peer reviewed.

Abstract

This research note documents a reconstructed regional time series of South Korea Housing and Urban Guarantee Corporation (HUG) presale-guarantee issuance records covering 2009 through the first half of 2026. The verified source contains 818 observations. The reconstruction preserves negative source values and missing regional observations rather than silently replacing them, and separately flags an ambiguous 2023 Q4 region label. The note is designed as a reproducible housing-finance and data-quality reference, not as a causal market analysis.

1. Research question

How can HUG regional presale-guarantee issuance data be reorganized into a reproducible time series while preserving source anomalies and avoiding interpretations that the source cannot support?

2. Source and coverage

The official source is HUG's presale-guarantee issuance file published through Korea's public data portal. The verified source contains 818 rows and four fields: period, region, guarantee issuance amount, and household count. Records are annual for 2009–2015 and quarterly from 2016 through 2026 Q2.

These aggregates use records that can be clearly assigned to the standard 17 regions. The 2023 Q4 source label “전라” is preserved but excluded from the standard-region aggregate because it cannot be allocated without additional evidence. The 2026 figure covers only the first two quarters and must not be compared mechanically with full-year totals.

4. Data-quality findings

The verified source contains 13 rows with negative guarantee-issuance values, 3 rows with negative household counts, 21 rows with zero household counts, and one non-standard regional label. These values are preserved and flagged rather than deleted or imputed. Missing regional rows are not treated as zero observations.

5. Interpretation limits

HUG guarantee issuance is not the same as apartment transaction value, completed presale contract value, or market price. Dividing guarantee issuance by recorded households creates a simple ratio but does not produce an average apartment presale price. Differences in guarantee coverage, project composition, timing, and source completeness prevent that interpretation.

6. Reproducibility

The public release contains the cleaned 818-row table, period and quarter summaries, quality flags, methodology, data dictionary, and citation metadata.

7. Research use

The series can support descriptive work on regional housing-finance activity, institutional guarantee flows, period comparisons, and data-quality auditing. Any market interpretation should be paired with additional housing supply, subscription, price, or transaction data that match the research question.
