# Institutional Backlink Operating Playbook

Updated: 2026-09-26

## Goal
Earn editorial links from universities, financial-education providers, research organizations, and public-interest information pages by offering reproducible housing-finance and DSR data.

## Primary assets
- https://resimanor.com/housing-finance-dsr-data/
- https://resimanor.com/citation-policy/

## Qualification
A target is worth pursuing when at least two are true:
1. It already links to private finance/real-estate tools or data services.
2. It teaches housing finance, DSR/LTV, proptech, asset management, or real-estate analysis.
3. It maintains a resource or related-sites page.
4. It has an editor-facing contact path such as department office, course proposal, Q&A, or program manager.

## Preferred acquisition paths
1. Existing related-sites/resource page
2. Broken/outdated link replacement
3. Educational supplementary material
4. Course/resource proposal
5. Research or journalism citation

## Exclude
- User-generated boards
- Profile/directory links
- Paid guest posts
- Link exchanges
- Automated backlink packages
- Mass outreach

## Suggested institutional labels
- 스트레스 DSR 데이터센터
- 주택금융·DSR 데이터

## Broken-link workflow
1. Find an institutional page containing an old housing-finance or real-estate service URL.
2. Verify that the old service is inaccessible, discontinued, or no longer relevant.
3. Match the replacement to the original intent.
4. Suggest the DSR data center as the canonical resource.
5. Re-check the institutional page after the next crawl/indexing cycle.

## Success criteria
A successful link should be:
- editorially placed by the institution;
- contextually relevant;
- on a page that is indexable;
- pointing to a canonical resource page;
- acquired without payment, exchange, or mass submission.
