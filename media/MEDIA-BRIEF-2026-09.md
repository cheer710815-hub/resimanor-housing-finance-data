# Media Brief — Korea Stress DSR and Mortgage Reference Data (September 2026)

Updated: 2026-09-26

## What this brief provides

A compact, reusable reference for journalists, educators, analysts, and developers covering Korean stress DSR and mortgage-limit examples.

## Current policy points

- Stress DSR adds a stress rate when calculating DSR; it does not directly raise the contractual loan rate.
- The Financial Services Commission applies a 3.0% stress-rate floor to mortgages in the capital area / regulated areas under the current framework.
- Credit loans are subject to stress DSR when the total credit-loan balance exceeds KRW 100 million.
- Actual approved mortgage limits can also be affected by LTV, existing debt, recognized income, maturity, repayment type, and lender underwriting.
