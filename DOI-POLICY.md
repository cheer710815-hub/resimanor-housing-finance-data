## Rule for future deposits

Do not deposit the exact same dataset package in additional DOI-minting repositories solely to gain more identifiers, backlinks, or discovery surfaces.

Use additional DOI repositories only when at least one of the following is true:

- the research object is materially different;
- the dataset is a new version with substantive changes;
- the deposit is a separately scoped derivative;
- the repository provides a distinct curation or preservation function and the relationship to the canonical object is clearly documented.

For discovery and backlinks, prefer services that index, catalog, cite, mirror without minting a competing DOI, or link to the canonical DOI.
