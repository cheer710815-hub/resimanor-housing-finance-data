# Research publication and citation policy (audit draft)

Interim policy as of 2026-11-24. No new DOI or provider deposit is authorized.

1. One research family and version must have a clear primary citation; legacy cross-deposits are transparently identified. Do not mint parallel DOIs for substantially identical files without explicit provider allowance and a documented need.
2. Zenodo/Figshare submissions remain on HOLD during account-restriction and integrity reviews. Never create alternate accounts to evade suspension.
3. Separate official policy reference tables, modeled DSR scenarios, empirical K-apt research, and pilots. A changed model assumption is not by itself proof of a new academically independent contribution.
4. Before publication, document original source and reuse license, dates, exact sample/units, calculations, code, independent reproduction checks, limits, previous related studies, AI/human responsibility, and human editorial approval.
5. Preserve every existing archived file, historical citation and correction log. Clarify current repository text without pretending frozen DOI archives were modified.
6. No bulk DOI releases, mass syndication, automated repeated access, or keyword-driven academic deposits.
7. Research data may support explanatory website posts, but commercial blog objectives are distinct from scholarly repository eligibility.

Cross-reference RESEARCH_INVENTORY.md and DOI_REGISTRY.md; all pending checks remain unresolved until evidenced.
